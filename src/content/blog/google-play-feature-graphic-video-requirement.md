**Key points:**
- A feature graphic is required to publish a Google Play store listing at all. Video or no video.
- It's a 1024x500 px JPEG or 24-bit PNG, no transparency, up to 15MB.
- To show a promo video on your listing, you also need a feature graphic. It works as the video's cover image.
- It also shows in collections and ad placements across Google Play, with no video involved at all.

The confusion is common. Does a feature graphic only matter once you add a video? No. Google Play requires one either way. The video dependency runs the other direction.

## What actually requires what

Reporting on Google's own [Play Console Help guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en) is consistent on this. A feature graphic is mandatory to publish a store listing. Full stop. A promo video is optional. It only shows on your listing if a feature graphic already exists, since it serves as the video's cover image.

| Asset | Required to publish a listing? | Depends on the other asset? |
|---|---|---|
| Feature graphic | Yes, always | No |
| Promo video | No, optional | Yes, needs a feature graphic present |

So the two aren't a package deal in the way many developers assume. You can ship a feature graphic with zero video. You cannot ship a video without a feature graphic behind it.

## Why it still matters with no video at all

An app with no promo video still gets real use out of its feature graphic. It shows up in large-format placements across Google Play. Collections, cross-promotion slots, ad units. None of that needs a video to exist. Treat the feature graphic as a video accessory, and you miss what it does on its own. Often in spots where a shopper never even sees your video-less listing directly.

> **Real-world scenario:** A team building a utility app skipped their feature graphic for months, assuming it was pointless without a promo video on the roadmap. Checking Google's own listing guidance directly showed the graphic was required to publish at all, not a video-only extra. They'd actually had a placeholder image sitting there the whole time, never designed with any collection or ad placement in mind. Once they treated it as its own asset, worth real design attention independent of any video, they rebuilt it around their actual value proposition instead of a generic app screenshot cropped to fit.

## What to actually do about it

1. **Design your feature graphic on its own merits.** It's required either way. It shows up in places a video never will.
2. **Add a promo video only once your feature graphic already earns its spot.** The video depends on it. Not the reverse. A weak graphic with a great video still under-serves you everywhere the video doesn't show.
3. **Check where your feature graphic is actually appearing.** Collections and ad placements are real distribution. Not a maybe.
4. **Re-verify current specs before you design.** Dimensions and file rules are worth confirming directly in your own Play Console. Not from any single summary, this one included.
