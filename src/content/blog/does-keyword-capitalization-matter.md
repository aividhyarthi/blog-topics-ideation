**Key points:**
- Search matching for a word doesn't seem to depend on how you capitalize it in your listing.
- A user's own search almost never matches your exact capitalization anyway, a sign the system doesn't care about case.
- Capitalization is still a real, separate choice. One about how professional or readable your listing looks to a human.
- The real lever worth your time is which words you pick, not how any single word is cased.

A visual choice about capitalization gets mistaken for a technical lever more often than it should.

## Why capitalization probably isn't the lever

Search tends to treat a word the same no matter how it's cased. "App," "app," and "APP" usually match as the same term. That fits how people actually search. Nobody types a query in a specific case expecting a different result than if they'd typed it another way. If the system cared about case, normal search would break constantly, since almost nobody searches with case in mind at all.

| What people try | What it's actually doing |
|---|---|
| ALL CAPS keyword | A visual choice, not a search-matching change |
| Title Case keyword | Same |
| lowercase keyword | Same |

## Why it's an easy thing to obsess over

Capitalization is visible, easy to change, and feels like a safe lever to pull. That makes it a tempting place to spend time when the real work, picking the right words in the first place, feels harder and less certain. Fiddling with case feels productive, even when it's likely not changing how your listing actually performs in search.

> **Real-world scenario:** A team spent a review cycle debating whether their app title should use title case or all lowercase, as if the choice might shift their keyword matching. After making the switch, their search performance for the same terms looked exactly the same as before, which is what you'd expect given how matching usually works. The time spent on that call was time not spent on the thing that would have actually mattered: whether the words themselves were right for how their real users search.

## What actually deserves that attention instead

Whether a word belongs in your listing at all is the real question. Is it something real users actually search for? Does it genuinely describe what your app does? Those questions carry real weight. How that same word is capitalized almost certainly doesn't. Put the time you'd spend agonizing over case into checking whether your word choices are right instead.

## What to actually do about it

1. **Stop treating capitalization as a search-matching lever.** There's no real reason to expect it changes how your keywords get matched.
2. **Use capitalization for readability and branding instead.** That's a fair, separate reason to make a deliberate choice here, just not a search one.
3. **Spend the time you'd use on casing checking your actual word choices.** Whether a term is relevant and searched for matters far more than how it's cased.
4. **Revisit your keyword list itself, not its formatting.** That's where real, measurable gains are more likely to come from.
