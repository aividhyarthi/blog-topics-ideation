**Key points:**
- Google's own [developer blog post](https://developer.android.com/blog/posts/accelerating-your-insights-with-faster-smarter-monetization-data-and-recommendations) confirms Sales Channel data, attributing revenue to the surface it came from: your app, the Play Store, or platforms like Google Play Games on PC.
- A new insights carousel on the Monetize with Play overview page tracks cart conversion rate, subscription cancellations, ARPPU, and engaged-to-buyer conversion.
- Play Console now surfaces its own recommendations too, each with an effort level and, where available, an estimated ROI.
- A separate Orders API gives programmatic access to order transaction details, for pulling real-time data into your own systems.

A dashboard most teams check once a month just got four real additions worth knowing about, straight from Google's own announcement.

## What Google's own post actually confirms

Per Google's own words, Sales Channel data shows which surface your revenue came from. Your app. The Play Store. Platforms like Google Play Games on PC. That matters if you run a game or subscription business across more than one surface. It answers a real question. Which channel is actually driving growth. Not just your total revenue.

| Detail | What Google's own post confirms |
|---|---|
| Sales Channel data | Revenue attributed by surface: app, Play Store, PC |
| Insights carousel metrics | Cart conversion rate, subscription cancellations, ARPPU, buyer conversion |
| Recommendations | Effort level, plus estimated ROI where available |
| Orders API | Programmatic access to order transaction details |

## Why the insights carousel is worth a real look

The new carousel sits on the Monetize with Play overview page. Right beside your core numbers. Google's own words call it a way to show "the visible and invisible value Google Play delivers to your bottom line." Four things get tracked. Cart conversion rate. Cancelled subscriptions over time. Average revenue per paying user. How much of your engaged audience actually becomes a buyer. None of this is a new idea. What's new is having it all in one place. No building your own tracking for each one.

> **Real-world scenario:** A subscription app's growth lead used to pull cart conversion and ARPPU by hand, from separate reports, every single month. She'd stitch them into a spreadsheet just to spot a trend. After Google's new carousel rolled out, that same comparison sat on one screen already. The real win wasn't new data. They'd always had access to it. It was not losing half a day each month just to assemble it, before any real work could start.

## What the Orders API actually replaces

Before this, pulling order details into your own system meant checking Play Console by hand. Or building a workaround. The Orders API gives direct, programmatic access to one-time and recurring order details instead. Per Google's own post, one studio said it "works so well that we want every app to use it." A real endorsement. Though it's one quoted developer. Not an independent benchmark.

## What to actually do about it

1. **Check your Monetize with Play page for the new carousel.** If it's not there yet, Google's own post says it's still rolling out.
2. **Use Sales Channel data if you sell across more than one surface.** It's the clearest new way to see which channel is actually worth your money.
3. **Treat recommendations as a starting point. Not a final answer.** Google's own post doesn't say how ROI estimates get calculated. Check any suggested action against your own numbers first.
4. **Integrate the Orders API if you still check order data by hand.** It's built to feed your own dashboards. Not to replace Play Console's own reports.
