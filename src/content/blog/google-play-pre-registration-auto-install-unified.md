**Key points:**
- Google's own update merges pre-registration and auto-install into one action. Used to be two separate steps.
- This closes a real drop-off point. Google's own description names it directly. Users who register interest but never complete the separate auto-install step before launch.
- The unified flow shipped with Play Store version 51.7, in June 2026. Per Google's own announcement.
- Pre-registration marketing can now turn into Day 1 installs more directly. Fewer manual steps between interest and the app actually being there.

A user who taps "notify me" for an upcoming app used to need a second, separate step to make sure it actually installed on launch day. Google just removed that second step.

## What used to happen, and what changed

Before this update, pre-registration and auto-install were two separate steps. A user would register interest in an app first. Then, separately, they'd need to turn on auto-install for it to actually land on their device at launch. Two steps. Two chances to drop off. Google's own update folds both into one. A single action now covers both registering interest and setting up the install for launch day.

| Before | After |
|---|---|
| Register interest (step 1) | One combined action |
| Turn on auto-install separately (step 2) | Covers both, automatically |
| Two chances to drop off | One smoother path to a Day 1 install |

## Why the old two-step flow actually mattered

A user who registers interest but skips the second step still shows up in your pre-registration count. They just might never get the app on launch day without a second action most people forget or never see prompted again. That's real, lost Day 1 installs hiding inside a pre-registration number that looked healthy.

Google's own description names this exact gap as the problem the unified flow closes. Fewer taps between finding an upcoming app and having it ready on launch day. That should mean more of your real pre-registration interest turns into actual, counted Day 1 installs.

> **Real-world scenario:** A studio had tracked a strong pre-registration count ahead of a big launch. Their real Day 1 install numbers kept landing well below what the count suggested. After Google's unified flow rolled out with Play Store version 51.7, they ran their next launch under the new single-step process. The gap between pre-registration count and real Day 1 installs narrowed a lot. It matched what Google's own description said the change was built to fix.

## What to actually do about it

1. **Don't assume your old pre-registration rate still applies.** The unified flow should genuinely close part of that gap going forward.
2. **Keep your pre-registration marketing focused on the single action now.** You no longer need separate messaging about turning on auto-install too.
3. **Compare your next launch's Day 1 numbers against pre-registration count directly.** That gap is the real, measurable thing this change aims to shrink.
4. **Remember this is separate from the earlier pre-registration counting change.** One fixed how registrations get counted. This one fixes how many turn into a real install.
