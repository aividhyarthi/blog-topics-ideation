**Key points:**
- There's no confirmed evidence an editorial badge like this feeds directly into keyword-matching math.
- The real effect is a separate exposure channel: curated placement in front of people who weren't searching your keywords.
- It's close enough to a general "featured" placement to plan for the same way.
- There's no reliable self-serve path to request it. It tends to follow apps already strong on quality and ratings.

A curated badge on your listing looks like it should be a direct ranking signal. The more honest answer is that it does a different job entirely.

## Why this isn't really a ranking question

Keyword rank comes from matching a search term against your listing's text and signals. An editorial badge comes from a real person at the store choosing to highlight your app. Those are two different things. There's no confirmed evidence the second one feeds into the first. Getting curated doesn't seem to hand you a bonus in the keyword math itself.

| | What actually drives it |
|---|---|
| Keyword rank | Matching and signals, computed per search |
| Editorial curation | A human choice to highlight your app |

## What it's actually doing instead

The real value is a second, separate channel. A curated spot puts your app in front of people browsing a featured section. Not people who typed a search term. Those are two different audiences, reached two different ways. One doesn't replace the other. A strong curated spot can bring real traffic without moving your keyword rank for anyone searching directly.

> **Real-world scenario:** A team landed an editorial placement and watched install numbers rise. They assumed this meant their keyword rankings had improved too. Checking their actual rank history for the same period showed no real shift at all. The extra installs had come from the curated spot itself. A real, valuable result on its own. Just not proof their regular search rank had moved.

## What to actually do about it

1. **Don't treat editorial curation as a keyword strategy.** There's no confirmed link between the two.
2. **Track your keyword rank and your curated-placement traffic as separate numbers.** Combining them hides what each one is actually doing.
3. **Focus on the basics that tend to attract curation.** Quality, ratings, and a strong listing are the closest thing to a real lever here.
4. **Treat a curated spot as a bonus channel, not a ranking fix.** It's real exposure. Just not the same kind as a keyword win.
